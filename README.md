INVERN
A puzzling platformer or a platform puzzler.
Players at any point may:
I) Turn platforms on/off
II) Flip Gravity Up/Down (x2 limit!)
	- think double jump rules, just gravity flipping

CONTROLS
Q ~ Switch Worlds
< ~ Move Left
> ~ Move Right
^ ~ Invern Gravity
v ~ Unvern Gravity
